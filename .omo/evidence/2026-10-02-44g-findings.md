## Error taxonomy and capture timing

- `TOOL_PERMISSION_DENIED`: SUCCESS envelope with nonempty `denied_actions`;
  denial details survive and empty-response retries do not run.
- `EMPTY_RESPONSE`: blank successful body, distinct from malformed/prose output.
  The existing `invoke()` transport retains its bounded three retries.
- `INVALID_RESPONSE_FORMAT`: successful envelope whose response is not a JSON
  object at the required packet boundary, including prose, malformed JSON,
  arrays, null and scalar JSON. Original response and model/role metadata survive.
- `INVALID_OUTPUT`: malformed outer AGY envelope; `MODEL_ERROR`: outer ERROR.
- `INVOCATION_FAILURE`: ledger transition status, carrying the failed call's
  evidence, not a substitute for the response classification.

The transcript predates the new packet classifier. Its captured `parsed.ok`
values describe outer-envelope parsing only; raw SyntaxError messages are
preserved rather than rewritten. The findings above apply the final taxonomy
to those raw responses. The final harness applies packet classification too,
so future empty provenance output is rejected rather than labeled OBSERVED.
